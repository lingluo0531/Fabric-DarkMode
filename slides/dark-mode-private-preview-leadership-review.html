Why Is Dark Mode Adoption Taking So Long?
Not a Functional Blocker
Dark mode does not block any user workflow — users can still operate fully in light mode. As a result, most workload teams do not treat dark mode as a high-priority item in their semester plans. While it is critical for Fabric Shell and the Unified UI vision, individual workload teams see it as incremental polish rather than a must-have deliverable.
Multi-Semester Tail Risk
Each team independently schedules onboarding into their own release windows. Adoption is fragmented across multiple semesters with no single forcing function to align all teams to a shared deadline, causing the rollout to stretch far beyond what a coordinated effort would require.
Prolonged UI Inconsistency
The longer the onboarding tail, the longer users encounter a mixed light/dark interface when navigating between workloads. This undermines perceived quality, erodes the premium feel of the platform, and turns a feature meant to improve UX into a visible gap in polish.

Concerns & Help Needed
Cross-Team Prioritization Gap
Several workload teams have deferred onboarding to Post-RB or later windows. Without explicit leadership direction to elevate dark mode priority across all teams, the gap between preview scope and full platform coverage will continue to grow.
Ask: Accelerate Onboarding to Public Preview
We need leadership backing to ensure all workload teams commit to a compressed, coordinated timeline — reducing the onboarding tail and delivering a consistent dark mode experience to customers without a prolonged period of UI inconsistency.
